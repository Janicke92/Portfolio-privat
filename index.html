About me

I'm a Front-End Development student at Noroff School of Technology and Digital Media, currently in the final part of my studies. Through my studies, I've worked with JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, APIs, Git and GitHub, as well as tools and workflows used in modern web development.

I enjoy building user-friendly applications and learning how the different parts of a web application work together. While my main focus is front-end development, I've also worked with back-end development, REST APIs and relational databases, which has given me a broader understanding of the development process. I'm particularly interested in continuing to develop my skills in React and TypeScript.

Alongside my studies, I work full-time in an elementary school and have several years of experience in leadership and staff management. Combining full-time work with part-time studies has taught me to manage my time, take responsibility for my own learning and stay focused when working on challenging problems.

I'm curious, collaborative and enjoy learning new technologies. I'm now looking forward to taking what I've learned through my studies and projects into my first professional role in development.
